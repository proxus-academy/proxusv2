Tests and flashcards both practise active recall, but they do not pose exactly the same challenge. The right format depends on what you want to learn.

## Use flashcards for specific answers

They work well for vocabulary, formulas, dates, classifications and relationships that can be expressed as a short question and answer.

## Use tests to discriminate

Multiple-choice questions force you to distinguish between similar answers. They are especially useful when the real exam uses the same format.

## Combine both formats

Start with flashcards to build a foundation and use tests to check whether you can apply and distinguish concepts. Test mistakes can become new review cards.
