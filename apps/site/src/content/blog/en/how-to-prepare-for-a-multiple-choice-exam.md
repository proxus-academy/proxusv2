Preparing for a multiple-choice exam is not just about rereading the syllabus. You need to retrieve information without seeing it, check your answer and decide what to review next.

## Start with a map of the syllabus

Break the content into small blocks and write down the concepts you should be able to explain in each one. Use this list as your reference for checking progress.

## Practise active recall

Close your notes and try to answer specific questions. Then compare your answer with the original material. The effort of remembering is part of learning.

## Analyse your mistakes

Do more than count correct answers. Classify each mistake: missing knowledge, confusion between concepts, rushed reading or overconfidence. Each type needs a different correction.

## Alternate practice and review

Return to weak points, leave some time between attempts and try again. Several short sessions usually reveal more than one marathon session.
